About us
Kirenkai, established in 1992, is the only Aikido organization at the University of Tokyo which is belonging to Aikikai. Based on what we learned from Hiroshi Tada, 9th Dan Hombu Dojo Shihan, we practice under the first captain, Saiga Shihan.
In addition to understanding how to use your own body, we aim to move supplely through training that puts an emphasis on breathing control.
